Judge Q&A Defense Matrix

❓ "How is this different from existing VR games or training simulators?"
💡 Answer: "Most VR simulators are graphical walkthroughs with subjective grading. CBRS-X is a deterministic auditing engine. Every gas PPM threshold, suit seal duration, and decontamination dwell time is evaluated with millisecond accuracy against official NDRF SOPs, producing tamper-evident SHA-256 verified credentials."
❓ "Can low-end government computers in remote training camps run this?"
💡 Answer: "Yes. By utilizing Unity WebGL with Brotli compression and LOD shaders, it runs inside standard Chrome on basic laptops. Furthermore, our Docker 'Field Box' allows the entire backend and DB to run offline on a single rugged laptop acting as a local Wi-Fi hotspot."
❓ "How do you ensure certificates cannot be forged or faked?"
💡 Answer: "Every certificate embeds an immutable SHA-256 cryptographic digest computed from the session ID, trainee ID, final score, and millisecond timestamps. Training academies can verify authenticity offline simply by recomputing the hash."
❓ "How difficult is it to add new disaster scenarios (e.g., Radiation or Biohazards)?"
💡 Answer: "Our backend scoring engine is polymorphic. Hazard thresholds, detector types, and penalty weights are defined in modular JSON state machines. New scenarios (like radiological dirty bombs or bio-containment) plug in without refactoring the core pipeline."
